# 04 — CONOPS / Use Cases

MS-V is employed at the **squad / fireteam level** as a rapid obscuration tool to disrupt drone observation and targeting. It is always used **in combination with standard visual smoke** to achieve multispectral screening (visual + IR/thermal). Employment follows existing smoke grenade TTPs with minimal additional training.

## General Employment Principles

1. **Never employ MS-V alone** for MoE against FPV/fiber-optic drones — pair with AN-M8 or M83 visual smoke
2. **Typical pattern:** 2–3 MS-V + 1–2 visual smoke per event, thrown in overlapping coverage
3. **Wind:** Throw slightly **upwind** of protected position when possible
4. **Timeline:** Effective density at T+12–15 s; plan actions through T+135 s (120+ s burn)
5. **Authority:** Platoon sergeant or designated MS-V employment authority

## Use Case 1: Casualty Recovery Under Drone Observation

### Situation

A soldier is hit in the open. A drone (FPV or loitering) is observing or maneuvering for a follow-up strike.

### Employment

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Team identifies drone observation; requests MS-V screen | T−0 |
| 2 | Grenadier throws **2 MS-V + 1 AN-M8** at casualty/LZ area | T+0 |
| 3 | Cloud builds to effective multispectral density | T+12–15 s |
| 4 | Team moves to casualty under combined screen | T+15 s |
| 5 | Litter team extracts casualty; screen maintained | T+15–120 s |
| 6 | Team reaches cover before terminal phase | T+120 s |

### Goal

Break or degrade drone **visual + thermal lock** long enough to recover the casualty and move to cover.

### Coordination

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| MS-V (2×) | Multispectral core — IR + partial VIS |
| AN-M8 (1×) | Visual supplement — FPV video degradation |
| EW | Optional — jam RF-linked drones if present |
| Kinetic | If attack profile develops |

## Use Case 2: Breaking Contact / Exfil Under Drone Pressure

### Situation

Squad is engaged and needs to withdraw. Enemy drones provide observation and targeting support.

### Employment

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Squad leader initiates break contact | T−0 |
| 2 | Rear element throws **2–3 MS-V + 2 AN-M8** in rear arc | T+0 |
| 3 | Dense, longer-burning cloud (2+ min) builds | T+12–15 s |
| 4 | Bounding element withdraws under screen | T+15–120 s |
| 5 | Second volley thrown for successive bound if needed | T+90 s |
| 6 | Squad consolidates at rally point | T+180+ s |

### Goal

Mask movement and break drone locks during withdrawal. Successive screens cover multiple bounds.

### Notes

Use MS-V + visual smoke **in sequence** for extended withdrawal — do not plan single 120 s screen for 300+ m exfil without rescreen.

## Use Case 3: Masking Infil / Approach

### Situation

Small team must cross open ground or approach an objective while enemy drones loiter overhead.

### Employment

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Team identifies drone patrol pattern and approach corridor | T−60 s |
| 2 | Grenadier throws **2 MS-V + 1 AN-M8** ahead or to flank of movement axis | T+0 |
| 3 | Team moves through or behind screen | T+15–90 s |
| 4 | Team reaches covered position | T+90 s |

### Goal

Reduce observation time during open-ground movement. Particularly useful against **fiber-optic / FPV drones that are hard to jam**.

### Notes

Do not throw signal smoke alone — it will not defeat thermal channel. MS-V without visual smoke is insufficient for FPV MoE.

---

## Use Case 4: Screening During Repositioning / Bounding Overwatch

### Situation

Squad is bounding or repositioning under drone overwatch.

### Employment

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Lead element prepares to move to next position | T−0 |
| 2 | Lead throws **2 MS-V + 1 AN-M8** between current and next position | T+0 |
| 3 | Lead moves under screen | T+15–60 s |
| 4 | Trail element follows in same screen or throws second volley | T+60–120 s |

### Goal

Create temporary **windows of reduced drone effectiveness** for each bound.

---

## Use Case 5: Hasty Defense / Position Masking

### Situation

Squad occupies a hasty defensive position while drones actively search or observe the area.

### Employment

| Step | Action | Time |
|------|--------|------|
| 1 | Squad occupies position; drone contact confirmed | T−0 |
| 2 | **2 MS-V + 1–2 AN-M8** thrown around position perimeter | T+0 |
| 3 | Combined cloud degrades thermal and visual acquisition | T+12–15 s |
| 4 | Squad improves cover, sets security, prepares counter-UAS | T+15–120 s |

### Goal

Degrade drone thermal and visual acquisition long enough to improve the position without immediate engagement.

### Notes

MS-V thrown near friendly position degrades friendly thermal optics — brief mask if entering cloud; position improvement should occur at position edge, not center of cloud.

## Employment Notes

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| MS-V alone | Insufficient for MoE — always pair with visual smoke |
| Typical volley | 2–3 MS-V + 1–2 visual smoke per event |
| Wind | Throw upwind; doctrine must emphasize wind awareness |
| Duration planning | 120+ s effective window; rescreen for longer actions |
| Fiber-optic drones | Obscuration primary defeat — EW ineffective |
| FPV drones | Combined MS-V + visual smoke degrades both channels |

## General Timeline Reference

```
T+0 s      Pin pull, throw (MS-V + visual smoke pattern)
T+2 s      Ignition
T+2–12 s   Streamer + build-up
T+12–15 s  Effective density — MOVEMENT WINDOW OPENS
T+15–135 s Effective screening (120+ s at good thickness)
T+135 s+   Terminal phase — rescreen if needed
```
