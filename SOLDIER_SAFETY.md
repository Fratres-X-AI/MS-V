# Soldier safety — read this before any other MS-V page

**Date:** Tuesday, 22 September 2026  
**Order:** Soldier safety, then reputation. If a number is not measured on a real cloud, it is not a procedure.

MS-V is a literature-parameter model of a grenade that has not been built. Nothing in this repository clears a soldier to throw it, breathe it, or move through it.

## Closed until a lab says otherwise

| Item | What the repo actually has | What you may not say |
|------|----------------------------|----------------------|
| **Toxicology (KPP-12)** | Unverified. No panel, no fill, no dose. | “Non-lethal.” “Acceptable irritation.” “Brief exposure is tolerable.” “Mask and go.” |
| **Issue to a soldier (KPP-14)** | A logistics wish: 1–2 per person. | That it is cleared to carry or throw. |
| **Throw (KPP-08)** | The published p10 of **20.0 m** was floored at the pass line. A 1 m combat-load penalty was subtracted and then deleted. Fixed in code on 22 Sep 2026. The 140M files still contain the floored number. | “Stressed throw is 20 m.” |
| **Fuze (KPP-07)** | Delays are random draws inside 0.7–2.0 s, then checked against that same window. | That an M201A1 fuze was tested. |
| **Wind (KPP-11)** | High-wind duration in the model stays ~170 s, the same as calm. The model is not showing wind. | That soldiers have cover above 15 mph. |
| **Tri-band (KPP-06)** | Reported VIS/NIR/MWIR transmittance is on the order of 1e-34 to 1e-54. That is a saturated equation, not a cloud. | That thermal and near-IR are defeated. |
| **Casualty movement (MOE-02)** | Model lock-met on casualty recovery is **14.9%**. Friendly thermal blackout in that case is **70.5%**. | That a squad can move to a casualty at T+15 s under cover. |
| **Duration ~170 s / “2 minutes”** | Model p10 on the nominal 3-grenade job. Visual-smoke and yield sweeps do not move it. Burn rate does. Burn rate has not been measured. | That a soldier has two minutes of cover. |

## What a YES in the matrix means

A YES means the **model** stayed on the passing side of a threshold inside literature bounds. It is not a field result. Duration above 120 s in the model is the only large YES left, and it is still not cover time.

## What March can be

A small burn, three cameras (visible, near-infrared, thermal), and a measured time. Not a pin-pull grenade, not a fuze, not a toxicity clearance, not a 20 m throw claim.

## Friendly troops

The model’s own CONOPS table puts friendly thermal blackout near **70%** on four of five use cases. Treat the cloud as blinding to your own thermal sights until a range says otherwise. Do not write a drill that sends people into the cloud.

## Decision revoked

**D-005** (“respiratory irritation acceptable, non-lethal”) was marked Locked. That lock is revoked as of 22 Sep 2026. Irritation was a hope, not a measurement.
